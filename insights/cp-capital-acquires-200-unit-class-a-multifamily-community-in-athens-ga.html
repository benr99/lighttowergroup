CP Capital acquires 200-unit, class A multifamily community in Athens, Ga.

CP Capital has announced the acquisition of Ascent Athens, a 200-unit, class A garden-style multifamily community in Athens, Ga., currently operating at 95 perc

Ben Rohr August 28, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

CP Capital, a U.S. real estate manager specializing in multifamily investments, has announced the acquisition of Ascent Athens, a 200-unit, class A garden-style multifamily community located in Athens, Ga. The property is currently operating at 95 percent occupancy. Developed by Westplan in 2020, the fully amenitized community offers residents a pool, fitness center, clubhouse and business center.

The investment is anchored by the University of Georgia, a 43,000-student institution with 11,500 faculty and staff that generates $8.4 billion in annual economic impact for the state. CP Capital also cited a diversifying local employment base and near-term supply constraints as factors supporting the acquisition. Jay Remillard, executive managing director at CP Capital, said the property represents "exactly the kind of opportunity we look for: the highest quality multifamily asset in the submarket, with no like-and-kind competition."

The acquisition suggests CP Capital is positioning around durable, university-driven rental demand in a submarket where new supply is constrained. The 95 percent occupancy figure indicates current tenant demand is strong, though the dossier does not provide historical occupancy trends, rental rates, or comparable transaction data to assess whether that performance is stable or improving.

What remains unknown is the purchase price, financing structure, capitalization rate, and any planned capital improvements or operational changes. The evidence is limited to a single secondary feed summary, so the transaction's financial terms and competitive context cannot be independently verified from the available sourcing.
