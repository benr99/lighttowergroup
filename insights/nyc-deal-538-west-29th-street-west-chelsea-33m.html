Why 540 West 29 LLC Just Paid $33.5 million for 538 West 29th Street in West Chelsea

The $33.5 million closing in West Chelsea sets a fresh $745 a square foot benchmark across 44,964 square feet.

Half a block from the High Line and one block south of Hudson Yards on West 29th Street between 10th and 11th avenues, assembling three contiguous C6-3 parcels unlocks architectural views that stretch to the Hudson River. On August 20, 2026, 540 West 29 LLC wired $33.5 million all-cash ($745 per buildable square foot across the three-parcel assemblage) to acquire 538–540 West 29th Street from W29 Owner LLC, more than tripling the $11 million price recorded on the lead parcel in March 2019. When the deed closed on August 20, 2026, 540 West 29 LLC had officially taken down 538 West 29th Street from W29 Owner LLC for $33.5 million, with Manhattan Land & Air Rights Advisory brokering the trade.

Look past the sticker price and the trade tells a clear story about replacement cost in Manhattan. At $745 a square foot across 44,964 square feet, the closing price marks a 204.5 percent step-up over the $11 million basis recorded in March 2019. In today's five-borough construction market, buying modern or well-maintained brick at $745 a square foot still beats the time and cost of digging a foundation from scratch.

All-Cash at $745 Per Buildable Foot Next to the High Line

Just as telling as the purchase price is what did not hit the city register alongside the deed: a senior mortgage. 540 West 29 LLC closed 538 West 29th Street with $33.5 million of unencumbered equity—or an unsecured corporate line—allowing the buyers to move with immediate execution certainty.

When financing contingencies can stall a New York closing for sixty days, showing up with $33.5 million in cash at $745 a square foot is the sharpest weapon a buyer can bring to the negotiating table—leaving the sponsorship free to recapitalize in the senior debt market once initial leasing and business-plan milestones are locked in.

From Our Desk

When we look at 538 West 29th Street on our placement desk at Light Tower Group, the lesson for New York sponsors comes down to the marriage of entry basis and capital structure. 540 West 29 LLC didn't try to fight the tape—they bought West Chelsea footage at $745 a square foot and structured the stack to match the asset's timeline. Closing a $33.5 million West Chelsea development site with 100 percent cash on the barrelhead is the cleanest way to fast-track foundation permits before bringing in a senior construction lender.
