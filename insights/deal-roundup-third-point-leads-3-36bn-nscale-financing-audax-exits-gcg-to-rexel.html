Deal Roundup: Third Point leads $3.36bn Nscale financing; Audax exits GCG to Rexel for $1.4bn

Third Point has led a $3.36bn pre-IPO convertible financing for British AI infrastructure business Nscale, with Nvidia, Apollo-managed funds, Citadel, Hudson Ba

Third Point has led a $3.36 billion pre-IPO convertible financing for Nscale, a British AI infrastructure business. The investor group includes Nvidia, funds managed by Apollo, Citadel, Hudson Bay Capital, Abu Dhabi Investment Council and 8090 Industries, according to a single summary from AltAssets Private Equity News. The same report also notes that Audax exited GCG to Rexel for $1.4 billion, though the summary provides no further detail on that transaction beyond the headline figure.

The Nscale financing is described as pre-IPO convertible, meaning the capital was raised ahead of a potential public listing and structured as convertible instruments rather than common equity. The presence of Nvidia alongside financial investors such as Apollo-managed funds, Citadel and Hudson Bay Capital indicates participation from both strategic and institutional sources. Abu Dhabi Investment Council and 8090 Industries round out the named investor group. The report does not specify the conversion terms, valuation, use of proceeds, or expected IPO timeline for Nscale.

The size of the Nscale financing, at $3.36 billion, is notable for a pre-IPO round in the AI infrastructure sector, where capital-intensive compute and data center buildouts have drawn large investor commitments. The involvement of a strategic chipmaker and multiple large asset managers is consistent with broader interest in AI infrastructure, but the dossier does not provide enough evidence to support conclusions about market trends, competitive positioning, or Nscale's financial condition. Any implication beyond the stated facts would exceed the available sourcing.

What remains unknown is substantial. The AltAssets source is a secondary, subscription-gated summary with only partial text available, and the dossier contains no material claims, missing-information fields, or additional facts beyond the headline and one-sentence summary. Details on the Audax-GCG-Rexel transaction, including structure, rationale, and closing status, are absent. For Nscale, the conversion mechanics, investor allocations, governance terms, and intended use of proceeds are not disclosed in the available evidence. The report should therefore be read as a deal announcement summary rather than a basis for deeper analysis.
