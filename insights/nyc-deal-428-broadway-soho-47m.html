SoHo Cast-Iron Flagship at 428 Broadway Commands $47M All-Cash—More Than Doubling Its 2005 Basis

The all-cash $47 million acquisition prices 428 Broadway at $1,119 a square foot and highlights the power of unlevered certainty.

On the cast-iron stretch of Broadway just north of Howard Street in SoHo, where global luxury houses and tech flagships fight for 16-foot ground-floor ceilings, full-building trades are generational events. 428 Broadway Owner LLC wired $47 million all-cash ($1,119 per square foot) on August 19, 2026 to acquire the six-story, 42,000-square-foot 1889 building at 428 Broadway from MS SoHo Holding LLC—more than doubling the $22.5 million basis recorded in February 2005.

In SoHo, scale and zoning scarcity drive the tape. Spanning 42,000 square feet on a 7,945-square-foot lot zoned M1-5/R9X, the property's $1,119 a square foot clearing basis delivers a 108.9 percent gain over its February 2005 sale at $22.5 million. Based on underwritten net operating income, the trade clears at a 5.80 percent going-in cap rate.

SoHo's M1-5/R9X Rezoning Meets a $47 Million All-Cash Check

Closing a $47 million SoHo flagship without a dollar of property-level mortgage debt is a statement of institutional conviction. By wiring 100 percent cash at closing, 428 Broadway Owner LLC captured a 5.80 percent going-in cap rate ($1,119 per gross square foot) without taking on floating-rate debt drag.

Even more compelling is the zoning tailwind: under the city's enacted M1-5/R9X SoHo/NoHo neighborhood rezoning, the 7,945-square-foot Broadway lot pairs world-class ground-floor luxury retail frontage with five upper floors of sun-drenched 1889 loft plates ripe for boutique office or residential conversion.
