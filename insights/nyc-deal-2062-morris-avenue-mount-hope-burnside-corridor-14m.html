Bronx Corner Assemblage at 2062 Morris Avenue & East Burnside Trades for $14M With Spencer Savings CEMA

The $14 million deed transfer pairs a $491 a square foot basis with $8.7 million in senior debt from Spencer Savings Bank, SLA.

At the bustling intersection of Morris Avenue and East Burnside Avenue in the West Bronx, foot traffic from the 4 and B/D subway stations keeps corner retail storefronts leased through every cycle. 101 E Burnside Holdings LLC closed on the three-parcel corner assemblage anchored by 2062 Morris Avenue and 101 East Burnside Avenue on August 31, 2026 for $14 million, marking a 42.1 percent step-up over the $9.85 million basis recorded in May 2022.

In Mount Hope / Burnside Corridor, scale and zoning scarcity drive the tape. Spanning 28,500 square feet on a 9,850-square-foot lot zoned C4-4D, the property's $491 a square foot clearing basis (or $583,333 per apartment across 18 residential units) delivers a 42.1 percent gain over its May 2022 sale at $9.85 million. Based on underwritten net operating income, the trade clears at a 6.40 percent going-in cap rate.

Spencer Savings Bank Rolls an $8.7 Million CEMA at 62 Percent LTV

To fund the $14 million purchase, New Jersey-based Spencer Savings Bank, SLA stepped up with an $8.7 million senior loan (62.1 percent loan-to-value) structured as an ACRIS Agreement (AGMT) consolidating the existing debt with $5.3 million of buyer equity.

Using a Consolidation, Extension, and Modification Agreement saved the sponsorship roughly $243,600 in New York City mortgage recording taxes at the closing table. Combined with C4-4D high-density commercial zoning and a 6.40 percent going-in cap rate from the Burnside Avenue retail corridor, the deal delivers immediate cash-on-cash yield.

For sponsors watching the Bronx tape from our desk at Light Tower Group, 101 E Burnside Holdings LLC's move at 2062 Morris Avenue sends a clear signal. By executing the $8.7 million Spencer Savings Bank loan via a CEMA consolidation, the buyer preserved roughly $240,000 in Bronx mortgage recording tax while locking in 62.1 percent leverage on a high-velocity retail corner.
