Blackstone targets $8.5bn for latest energy transition fund

Blackstone is targeting $8.5 billion for its latest energy transition fund, according to Private Equity International. The previous vintage closed on $5.58 bill

Ben Rohr September 23, 2026 1 min read 2 sources
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Blackstone is targeting $8.5 billion for its latest energy transition fund, according to a report by Private Equity International. The publication, citing its reporting on the private equity firm's fundraising activity, states that the new vehicle follows a previous vintage that closed on $5.58 billion in February last year. The target figure was reported on September 23, 2026, by both Private Equity International and its affiliated outlet New Private Markets.

The reported $8.5 billion target would represent a meaningful step-up from the prior fund's $5.58 billion close. Blackstone is the only company named in the available reporting. No additional details were provided in the retrieved text regarding the fund's strategy, limited partners, investment focus, or expected timeline for the close. The two sources identified in the dossier are both secondary outlets under PEI Media, and the available text is partial, consisting primarily of headline and summary information.

If Blackstone reaches the $8.5 billion target, the new fund would be roughly 52% larger than the previous vintage. However, the available evidence is limited to a single summary and does not include material claims, quotes, or corroborating detail beyond the two figures. As a result, the reporting supports only the fact of the target and the prior close; it does not establish whether the target reflects increased investor demand, a broader investment mandate, or other strategic considerations.

What remains unknown is substantial. The dossier does not specify the fund's formal name, its investment strategy within energy transition, the identities of any limited partners, or the timing of a first or final close. It is also unclear whether the $8.5 billion figure is a hard cap or a preliminary target, and whether the prior $5.58 billion close represented the final size of that vehicle. Until additional reporting or primary confirmation from Blackstone is available, the target should be treated as an early-stage fundraising signal rather than a completed outcome.
