BridgeInvest Refis Savannah Industrial Portfolio With $43M Loan

Thor Equities Group has sealed a $43.2 million loan to refinance an industrial portfolio in Savannah, Ga., Commercial Observer has learned.

Ben Rohr October 08, 2026 3 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Thor Equities Group has sealed a $43.2 million loan to refinance an industrial portfolio in Savannah, Ga., Commercial Observer has learned. BridgeInvest provided the loan via its transitional bridge program for the Ardley Portfolio, which consists of 11 warehouse buildings on 27 acres at 25-27 Artley Road. Thor Equities acquired the roughly 525,000 square feet of logistics space in 2022 for $49 million. The refinancing matters because it signals continued lender appetite for Savannah industrial assets tied to port and interstate infrastructure, even as the broader commercial real estate market faces scrutiny over valuations and financing costs.

The material facts are narrow but concrete. The loan amount is reported as $43.2 million, with the headline rounding to $43 million. The collateral is the Ardley Portfolio, an 11-building warehouse cluster totaling approximately 525,000 square feet on 27 acres. The property sits at 25-27 Artley Road, nine miles from the Port of Savannah and less than two miles from Interstate 95. Iron Hound Management negotiated the refinancing, though Iron Hound declined to comment. The reported acquisition price in 2022 was $49 million, which provides a rough reference point for how the new debt relates to the prior purchase, but the dossier does not state the current valuation, loan-to-value ratio, interest rate, term, or any other financing mechanics.

The evidence comes from a single full-text source: a Commercial Observer article by Andrew Coen published October 7, 2026. The report includes direct statements from two principals. Alex Horn, founder and managing partner at BridgeInvest, said: “We like lending against industrial developments where the location can’t be re-created, close to ports, interstates and airports, and sized for the local businesses that actually drive an economy.” He added: “Savannah is one of the clearest industrial stories in the country right now.” Joe Sitt, chairman of Thor Equities, said: “Closing this financing reflects the strength of the Savannah industrial market and the quality of the Artley Portfolio.” The tenant mix cited by Thor includes Thunder Logistics, CRS and TransRepublica, though occupancy, lease terms, and rent rolls are not disclosed in the source.

The sector implication is that Savannah industrial real estate remains a favored lending target for transitional bridge capital. BridgeInvest's stated focus on locations that “can't be re-created” near ports, interstates and airports aligns with the Ardley Portfolio's physical position relative to the Port of Savannah and I-95. The refinancing also suggests that a 2022 acquisition at $49 million can support a $43.2 million bridge loan in 2026, but without current appraised value or debt yield figures, the source does not establish whether the loan represents conservative leverage or aggressive underwriting. The presence of multiple logistics tenants indicates operational cash flow, yet the dossier provides no net operating income, vacancy, or lease expiration data.

Several limitations bound this analysis. The evidence level is single_full_text, meaning all claims rest on one secondary source read in full. The dossier does not include the loan's maturity, rate, recourse structure, or prepayment terms. It also does not state whether the refinancing pays off existing debt, returns equity to Thor, or funds capital improvements. The $49 million acquisition figure appears in the article but is not independently corroborated. What to watch: whether additional reporting confirms the loan terms, whether Savannah industrial rents and vacancy support the debt service, and whether BridgeInvest's transitional bridge program sees similar deals in other port-adjacent markets. Until those details emerge, the transaction is best read as a single data point confirming lender conviction in Savannah logistics, not as a broad market signal.
