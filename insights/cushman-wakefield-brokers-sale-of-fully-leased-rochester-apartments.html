Cushman & Wakefield Brokers Sale of Fully Leased Rochester Apartments

Cushman & Wakefield has brokered the $7.27 million sale of Cascade Apartments, a 44-unit multifamily community in Rochester, Minnesota.

Ben Rohr September 12, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Cushman & Wakefield has brokered the $7.27 million sale of Cascade Apartments, a 44-unit multifamily community in Rochester, Minnesota. The firm's Chris Collins, Lance Steiger, Evan Miller and Erin Salway represented the seller, Patina Management. The buyer was Black Swan Living. The property attracted eight competitive offers from buyers across Minnesota, Wisconsin and Illinois, as well as national investment groups, and closed in an all-cash transaction.

Located at 957 Pendant Lane NW, Cascade Apartments is a 36,913-square-foot multifamily property completed in 2016. The community features a mix of studio, one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments. At the time of sale, the property was fully occupied. Situated approximately 10 minutes from Mayo Clinic, the asset benefits from Rochester's economic foundation, which is anchored by healthcare, technology and research employers.

The competitive bidding and all-cash close support the view that Rochester multifamily assets remain attractive to regional and national investors. Collins said, "Investors continue to be attracted to Rochester's long-term fundamentals, driven by the presence of Mayo Clinic, a highly educated workforce and ongoing public and private investment throughout the market." The fully leased status at sale suggests stable in-place cash flow was a factor in buyer interest, though the dossier does not provide rent rolls, cap rates, or per-unit pricing.

What remains unknown is the financial structure beyond the all-cash nature of the transaction, the intended holding strategy of Black Swan Living, and whether the sale price reflects a premium or discount to Rochester multifamily benchmarks. The source also does not disclose the property's operating history, renovation plans, or how the eight offers compared on price and terms. Further reporting would be needed to assess whether this sale signals a broader trend in Rochester's multifamily investment market.
