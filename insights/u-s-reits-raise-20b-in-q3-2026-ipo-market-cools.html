U.S. REITs Raise $20B in Q3 2026; IPO Market Cools

Debt offerings made up $14.9 billion of the Q3 total.

Ben Rohr October 13, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Debt offerings made up $14.9 billion of the Q3 total. Common equity offerings added $4.7 billion, and preferred equity offerings added $173 million.

The REIT IPO market cooled with no offerings in Q3 after an active first half of 2026.

Three public U.S. REIT deals totaling $8.6 billion were announced in the quarter. Public-REIT to public-REIT deals accounted for $3.6 billion, or 42%, of that total. One take-private deal was announced over this period, valued at $4.9 billion.
