Staples Tower LLC Pays $26.85M for Astoria's 24-29 31st Street With $20M From Ponce Bank

Backed by a $20 million loan from Ponce Bank, N.A., the Astoria closing clears at $1,022 a square foot across 26,280 square feet.

Right beneath the elevated N and W subway trestle at the Astoria Boulevard station, single-story commercial footprints sitting on nearly 30,000 square feet of high-density C4-5X dirt have become the most coveted covered-land plays in western Queens. Staples Tower LLC wired $26.85 million on September 18, 2026 to acquire the longtime commercial corner at 24-29 31st Street from 2441 Astoria Associates, LLC, pricing the existing 26,280-square-foot retail structure at $1,022 per building foot while capturing roughly 150,000 square feet of as-of-right C4-5X mixed-use zoning capacity.

Across the property's 26,280 square feet on a 29,940-square-foot C4-5X lot, the $26.85 million trade establishes a $1,022 a square foot clearing mark for Astoria Astoria Boulevard Retail & Mixed-Use Development Corner product.

Ponce Bank Steps Up to 74.5 Percent Leverage for Covered Queens Dirt

While money-center banks routinely cap land and pre-development loans at 50 percent loan-to-cost, Bronx-headquartered relationship lender Ponce Bank, N.A. stepped up with a $20 million senior mortgage—representing 74.5 percent of the $26.85 million purchase price—leaving the buyers to fund just $6.85 million in closing equity.

The secret to unlocking 74.5 percent bank leverage on an Astoria redevelopment play is the existing 26,280-square-foot commercial building. Because national credit retail tenancy covers day-one debt service while architects draft vertical C4-5X tower plans, Ponce Bank could underwrite an income-producing retail asset rather than raw dirt.
