Weekly Wrap: Oct 10 to Oct 16

99 stories across 12 sectors this week. Here is what mattered, sector by sector.

Ben Rohr October 16, 2026 8 min read 50 sources
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

99 stories across 12 sectors this week. Here is what mattered, sector by sector.

Fed, Rates & Macro

  • Rate Hike Odds Jump to 47% After Inflation Surprise The probability that the Fed will raise rates jumped from 22% on Tuesday to 47% on Wednesday morning, according to CME Group’s FedWatch tool. (Bisnow National)
  • Inflation uptick puts pressure on Fed to hike again The CPI report is the last major economic indicator ahead of the FOMC's communications blackout period before its next meeting on Oct. (American Banker)
  • Inflation speeds up to 3.7%, fueled by surging gasoline prices Consumers this month expect inflation during the coming decade to average around 2.7%, the highest level since July 2007, the Cleveland Fed said Wednesday. (CFO Dive)
  • MBA expects two more Federal Reserve hikes, in December and Q1 2027 The MBA expects two more Fed hikes, one in December and another in Q1 2027, with 30-year mortgage rates staying above 7% through next year. (Mortgage Professional America)
  • Inflation Hits 3.7% as Gasoline Prices Surge Annual inflation hit 3.7% in September, up from 3.4% in August, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. (Connect CRE)
  • Bowman: global regulators should match Fed's 'modernization Bowman spoke Thursday at the Bangkok Economic Festival. (American Banker)

Debt & Capital Markets

  • ARMs, non-QM are the 4Q opportunities, rate lock data shows Non-QM locks rose 8% from August and ARMs hit their highest share since 2019, while total rate locks fell 26% year over year. (National Mortgage News)
  • Anderson Family Properties Lands $550M Refi for Century City Office Campus Wells Fargo led the financing with J.P. Morgan Chase. (Commercial Observer)
  • Brookfield-Backed Castlelake Dives Into CRE CLO Market With $1.1B Deal Castlelake is entering the CRE CLO market with a $1.1 billion issuance backed by floating-rate senior loans on 26 properties across multifamily, industrial, hospitality, self-storage, and student housing. Why it matters: Elevated CLO issuance since 2025 reflects property owners using bridge loans to manage higher debt service costs until rates fall. (Bisnow National)
  • OCC Imposes $750 Million Assessment Against Bank of America for Deficient BSA/AML Compliance The OCC imposed a $750 million civil money penalty on Bank of America for Bank Secrecy Act violations, the largest in OCC history. (OCC News Releases)
  • ABA, state bankers associations offer recommendations for CRA rules revisions The American Bankers Association and 45 state bankers associations said in a letter to the FDIC and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency that the small bank threshold should be increased to…… (ABA Banking Journal)
  • BofA gets $1.21B penalty for money laundering Bank of America failed to report over $5 billion in suspicious transactions, $1 billion of which reportedly had national security implications. (American Banker)

Multifamily

  • Where Is Multifamily Delinquency Concentrated? Private-Label CMBS Holds Half of Delinquent Debt Private-label CMBS holds $75.48 billion of multifamily debt with a 7.69% delinquency rate, while Freddie Mac's rate is 0.80%. Half of the $11.6 billion delinquent securitized multifamily loans sits in private-label CMBS. (Trepp Blog)
  • First Multifamily-Only Deal for Genesis Since 2021 Genesis Capital closed a $375 million securitization of multifamily loans, its first multifamily-only deal since Rithm Capital acquired it in 2021. (Commercial Observer)
  • JSB Capital Group Secures $238M Refinancing for Class A Multifamily Community in Miami Submarket JSB Capital Group received a $238 million refinancing loan from Torchlight Investors for The Landmark South, a 631-unit Class A community in Doral, Fla. (Yield PRO)
  • Walker & Dunlop Arranges $203M Financing for East Village Multifamily Walker & Dunlop arranged a $203.5 million floating-rate construction loan for 50 Third Ave., a 184-unit mixed-use development in Manhattan's East Village. (Connect CRE)
  • Financing Closes For Renovation Of Nostrand Houses In Sheepshead Bay, Brooklyn Financing closed for the $607 million modernization of Nostrand Houses, a 16-building public housing development in Sheepshead Bay, Brooklyn. (New York YIMBY)
  • Fuse Group Joint Venture Secures $127M Construction Loan for Miami-Area Multifamily Property Fuse Group and KREA Developments secured a $127 million construction take-out loan for The Arcadian, a 502-unit multifamily community in Fort Lauderdale. Why it matters: Proceeds will refinance existing debt and support lease-up and stabilization at the property. (Yield PRO)

Industrial & Warehouse

  • Soar AI Secures $1B Equity For Industrial-To-Data-Center Development Volato Group, the former private aviation company rebranding as Soar AI, struck a stock purchase agreement with an institutional investor for an equity facility of up to $1B. (Bisnow National)
  • Lake Forest Bank Refis Queens IOS Site With $39M Loan Sitex Group acquired an industrial outdoor storage property in Maspeth for $50 million in late 2021. (Commercial Observer)
  • Global Investor Buys 8 Mass. Industrial Buildings For $76.5M: The Boston Deal Sheet Longfellow had acquired the eight properties between 2021 and 2022 for roughly $62.3M, according to deed records. (Bisnow (all markets))
  • IPA Capital Markets Arranges $50M for 18-Property Industrial Portfolio IPA Capital Markets arranged $50M in non-recourse financing for an 18-property industrial portfolio across Texas, Oklahoma and Georgia. (Connect CRE)

Office

  • Starwood Property Trust Provides $238M Refi on N.C. Life Sciences Campus King Street Properties secured $237.7 million to refinance the first phase of Pathway Triangle. (Commercial Observer)
  • Terra Refinances Tom Brady-Leased Office Near Miami for $184M Terra refinanced its 96,000-square-foot office at the Well development in Bay Harbor Islands with a $183.8M loan from Monroe Capital. (Commercial Observer)
  • Chicago Office Tower Sells For $550M To Zara Founder In Blockbuster Deal Amancio Ortega, the founder of the fashion retailer Zara, reportedly acquired the 51-story office tower at 1 N. Wacker Drive for about $550M. (Bisnow (all markets))
  • One Street Gets $38M Loan for D.C. Office-to-Condo Conversion One Street Commercial Properties got a $37.8M loan from OakNorth and Hickory CRE Lending to convert a vacant D.C. office into 22 condos. (Commercial Observer)

Retail

  • CBL Properties Receives $135 Million Refinancing for St. Louis Mall CBL Properties refinanced West County Center in St. Louis with a $135 million fixed-rate loan at 7.4% for five years. Why it matters: The new loan removes the cash management sweep, adding an estimated $7 million in annual free cash flow. (Shopping Center Business)
  • Cohen & Steers Nontraded REIT Adds $92.3M in Shopping Centers as NAV Nears $392M Cohen & Steers Income Opportunities REIT has bought two open-air shopping centers for a combined $92.3 million. (The DI Wire)
  • PNC Lends $96M for Hawaii Retail Portfolio Acquisition 11North Partners got a $96 million loan from PNC Bank to buy two grocery-anchored retail properties in Lahaina, Hawaii, for $155 million. (Commercial Observer)

Hospitality

  • Braemar Hotels & Resorts to Sell Four Seasons Resort Scottsdale for $372M as Part of Self-Managed REIT Strategy Braemar Hotels & Resorts agreed to sell Four Seasons Resort Scottsdale for $372 million, or $1.8 million per key, to an undisclosed buyer. (RE Business Online)

Data Centers

  • Dell’Oro: Data Center Infrastructure Market Surges 27% Dell'Oro says data center physical infrastructure revenue hit $14 billion in Q2 2026, up 27% year-over-year. Why it matters: Thermal management grew over 40% as operators invested in direct liquid cooling, and busway availability is constraining new installations. (Converge Digest)
  • Solaria plans 500 MW data center project powered by 350 MW of solar in Uruguay Solaria plans a $5.7 billion data center park in Uruguay with 500 MW of data centers and 350 MW of solar. Why it matters: The solar capacity will not meet full demand, so additional power sources will be required. (PV Magazine)
  • AMD Backs 222 MW Fermi America Data Center Lease Fermi America signed a credit support agreement with AMD for a 222 MW first phase of Project Matador. (Converge Digest)
  • IURC approves AES Indiana plan for $1.3bn solar, storage, and transmission project for Google data center in Monrovia Indiana regulators approved AES Indiana's $1.3 billion solar, storage, and transmission project for a Google data center in Monrovia. (Data Center Dynamics)
  • DayOne secures $3.67bn in funding for Malaysia expansion DayOne secured $3.67bn in funding for Malaysia expansion from CIMB, Crédit Agricole CIB, DBS, Maybank, OCBC, Standard Chartered, and UOB. (Data Center Dynamics)
  • Microsoft acquires 600 acres of land for data center in Woodward, Iowa Microsoft acquired 600 acres in Woodward, Iowa, for a potential data center campus. Why it matters: The land was rezoned in June 2026, and at least 300 residents attended the meeting where one person was forcibly removed. (Data Center Dynamics)

Energy & Infrastructure

  • Trump goes after an offshore wind farm that’s already up and running The Interior Department asked a court to remand its 2023 approval for Revolution Wind, a $6.2 billion offshore wind farm already producing power off Connecticut and Rhode Island. (Canary Media)
  • $100M Private Capital Effort Launched to Address Baltimore Housing Issues The Greater Baltimore Committee plans to launch a new Funders' Consortium in November. (Yield PRO)
  • Oswal Pumps to build 1.2 GW TOPCon solar cell plant Oswal Pumps plans to establish a 1.2 GW solar cell manufacturing facility based on TOPCon technology. (PV Magazine)

Private Equity & Private Credit

  • Blackstone Tactical Opportunities exits ZO Skin Health in $1.5bn sale Blackstone Tactical Opportunities is selling ZO Skin Health to Galderma for $1.5bn enterprise value, an all-cash deal at about 4.5 times trailing revenue. Why it matters: Completion is expected in Q4 2026 or early 2027. (Alt Assets)
  • U.S. REITs Raise $20B in Q3 2026; IPO Market Cools Debt offerings made up $14.9 billion of the Q3 total. (Connect CRE)
  • Exclusive: Chariot Raises $10M To Help Consumers Fight Back Against Hidden Fees And Hard-To-Cancel Subscriptions Chariot Claims raised $10m Series A led by Silverton Partners, bringing total funding to $15.7m. The startup helps consumers pursue claims over hidden fees and hard-to-cancel subscriptions. (Crunchbase News)

Policy & Local Government

  • Housing Report: City Planning Commission Adds 670 Dwelling Units and a Long-Term Care Facility as Summer Ends The City Planning Commission approved about 670 dwelling units in September, including a 300-bed long-term care facility, and certified eight new projects. (CityLand NYC)
  • Horse-drawn carriages to be banned in Central Park after deal between Council and Mamdani City Council Speaker Julie Menin and Mayor Zohran Mamdani agreed on legislation to end horse-drawn carriages in Central Park by June 2028. (6sqft)

Other Commercial Real Estate

  • 2 Major H&R REIT Shareholders Plan To Vote To Block $5B Breakup RBC Global Asset Management and Mill Pond Capital plan to vote against H&R REIT's $4.8B breakup, holding 9.3M and 2.2M units. (Bisnow National)
  • EQT to Acquire Stockbridge, Adding US$16.5B in AUM EQT AB has agreed to acquire Stockbridge, a leading U.S. real estate investment manager with approximately US$16.5 billion in fee-generating assets under management. (Connect CRE)
  • BlackRock Invests In 3D-Printing Construction Startup Valued At $1.4B BlackRock and Baillie Gifford co-led a $200M Series D for Icon, valuing the 3D-printing construction startup at $1.4B. (Bisnow National)
  • Brookdale Senior Living To Buy 30 Communities It Operates From Welltower For $290M Brookdale Senior Living will buy 30 communities it leases from Welltower for $290M, covering 1,152 units. Why it matters: Brookdale also extended leases on 39 communities and gained a fair-market purchase option on that portfolio. (Bisnow National)
  • DPR breaks ground on $230M Kaiser Permanente project in Colorado DPR Construction broke ground on a $230M, 132,000-square-foot Kaiser Permanente medical office building in Westminster, Colorado. (Construction Dive)
  • Cannot stop the battery | Factor This Brief PJM claims roughly 686 MW of battery energy storage across 42 operating utility-scale projects. (Renewable Energy World)

Each item links to the full piece. The wrap summarises what we published this week; it is analysis of reported facts, not investment advice.
