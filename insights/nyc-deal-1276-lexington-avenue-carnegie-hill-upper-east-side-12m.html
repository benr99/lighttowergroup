At the corner of Lexington Avenue and East 86th Street—directly above the 4, 5, and 6 express subway stop in Carnegie Hill—six ground-floor retail storefronts beneath nine free-market apartments generate some of the most durable cash flow uptown. On August 26, 2026, 1276 Lexington Owner LLC closed on the five-story, 16,295-square-foot corner building at 1276 Lexington Avenue for $12.6 million ($773 per square foot) from 1276 Lex Owner LLC.

Run the numbers across 16,295 square feet and the buyer's thesis comes into view. At $773 a square foot (or $840,000 per apartment across 9 residential units) on a 3,550-square-foot parcel zoned C5-1A, 1276 Lexington Owner LLC is locking in irreplaceable Carnegie Hill / Upper East Side brick well below new-construction replacement cost. Based on underwritten net operating income, the trade clears at a 6.00 percent going-in cap rate.

A $9.75 Million CEMA Assignment Takes Leverage to 77.4 Percent

To finance the $12.6 million trade, EMG Transfer Agent LLC provided a $9.75 million senior loan structured as an ACRIS Agreement (AGMT), giving the buyer 77.4 percent loan-to-value against $2.85 million of implied cash equity.

Because the $9.75 million note was executed as a CEMA consolidation rather than a brand-new mortgage, the sponsorship saved roughly $273,000 in New York City mortgage recording taxes—nearly 10 percent of its entire cash equity check—while locking in a 6.00 percent going-in cap rate on an irreplaceable 86th-and-Lexington subway corner.

From Our Desk

Looking at 1276 Lexington Avenue from our debt and equity desk at Light Tower Group, one takeaway stands out for sponsors sizing up Carnegie Hill / Upper East Side. Executing the $9.75 million senior note via an ACRIS AGMT consolidation through EMG Transfer Agent LLC allowed the buyer to reach 77.4 percent leverage on a prime 86th-and-Lexington subway corner while shielding the deal from New York's 2.8 percent mortgage recording tax.
