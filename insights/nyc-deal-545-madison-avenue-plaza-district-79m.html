Marx Realty Takes Down 545 Madison Avenue for $79.5M, Resetting Plaza District Basis at $569/SF

The $79,500,000 deed transfer at 545 Madison Avenue pairs a $569-per-square-foot basis with $54,500,000 in senior financing from Peachtree Group (Credit Division) at 68.6% LTV.

In a market where bid-ask spreads have kept dozens of New York City investment sales on ice, Marx Realty (Craig Deitelzweig, President & CEO) and Merchants Hospitality via 545 Madison Owner LLC stepped in to close on 545 Madison Avenue in Plaza District, Manhattan for $79,500,000 ($79.5M). The fee-simple deed transfer, which closed on 2026-03-18 and hit the city register on 2026-03-25, prices the 139,600-square-foot class a office tower at $569/GSF, resetting the building's valuation 24.3% below its 2019 trade of $105,000,000 (6.8-year hold).

The Deal & The Players

Marx Realty (Craig Deitelzweig, President & CEO) and Merchants Hospitality via 545 Madison Owner LLC acquired the 100% interest from LCOR Incorporated via LCOR 545 Madison LLC, with Eastdil Secured (investment sales and debt placement) advising on the trade. ACRIS Document ID 2026032500412001 records the fee-simple transfer of 545 Madison Avenue (BBL 1-01290-0052) in Manhattan's Plaza District from LCOR 545 Madison LLC to 545 Madison Owner LLC, led by Marx Realty (Craig Deitelzweig) and Merchants Hospitality, for $79,500,000. Simultaneously recorded CEMA mortgage filing 2026032500412002 shows Peachtree Group providing a $54,500,000 senior acquisition and repositioning facility on the 139,600-square-foot, 18-story Class A office building built in 1955 at the corner of Madison Avenue and East 55th Street. The $79,500,000 purchase price resets the 139,600-square-foot tower's basis 24.3% below its $105,000,000 June 2019 benchmark, giving Marx Realty a $569-per-square-foot entry point on Madison Avenue with $25,000,000 of implied sponsor and JV equity.

The Capital Stack X-Ray

Rather than originating a scratch mortgage and absorbing New York City's full mortgage recording tax bite, the sponsorship structured the debt through a Consolidation, Extension, and Modification Agreement (CEMA, ACRIS Document ID 2026032500412002), preserving tax efficiency on the $54,500,000 facility from Peachtree Group (Credit Division). Sitting behind the senior note is $25,000,000 in implied equity ($25M), illustrating the disciplined 60% to 70% senior attachment points that are actually clearing New York City credit committees in 2026.

The Asset & Street Reality

Identified on the city tax map as BBL 1-01290-0052 under building class O4 (Class A Office Tower), 545 Madison Avenue spans 139,600 gross square feet across 18 stories on a 10,040-square-foot lot zoned C5-3 (MID) (built 1955).

The Desk Read

On our placement desk at Light Tower Group, 545 Madison Avenue is a textbook readout of how institutional capital is pricing New York City risk right now. First, clearing basis is everything: at $569/GSF, the sponsorship is buying Plaza District footage at a basis that insulates the business plan against rate volatility. Second, look at the debt execution: Peachtree Group (Credit Division) stepping up for $54.5M at 68.6% LTV proves that credit committees are wide open for right-sized basis and institutional sponsorship—especially when borrowers use CEMA structures or agency/life-company execution to protect day-one cash yield.
