WD Capital Nears $1B in Financings and Equity Placements

The Miami-based real estate capital markets advisory firm is approaching $1 billion in financings and equity placements just 24 months after founding, with majo

Ben Rohr October 04, 2026 2 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

WD Capital Group is nearing $1 billion in financings and equity placements since the firm's founding just 24 months ago, according to a Yield PRO report. The Miami-based real estate capital markets advisory firm describes the volume as significant, and the milestone matters because it signals how quickly a relatively new capital markets shop can scale in Florida's active construction and development environment. The reported placements span construction financing and mezzanine debt, two categories that have become more prominent as developers adjust to tighter senior lending conditions.

The material facts are limited but specific. Among the firm's major placements are a $101 million construction financing for Visions Orlando Resort & Spa, an $85.5 million construction loan for MIRAI Design District in Miami, and $50 million in mezzanine financing for the East Tower at Oasis Hallandale. The East Tower, designed by Arquitectonica, includes 250 one-, two- and three-bedroom residences and has topped out at 25 stories. It is one of two 25-story residential towers totaling 500 units within the broader 10-acre Oasis Hallandale development, which also includes roughly 60,000 square feet of retail and dining space and 35,000 square feet of office space across five commercial buildings.

The evidence comes from a single full-text source, Yield PRO, published on October 3, 2026. The dossier does not include corroborating documents, transaction-level term sheets, or public filings, so the dollar figures should be read as reported placements rather than independently verified totals. The source is a secondary trade publication, and the article does not provide a complete list of all transactions contributing to the near-$1 billion figure. That leaves the exact composition of the firm's cumulative volume partially unspecified beyond the three named deals.

The sector implication is that capital stacks are shifting. The Yield PRO report notes that developers increasingly rely on preferred equity, joint venture capital, mezzanine debt and family office capital to fill gaps created by lower senior leverage and more conservative underwriting. WD Capital's reported activity fits that pattern: the $50 million mezzanine placement for the East Tower is a direct example of subordinated capital being used within a large mixed-use project. For Florida developers and capital providers, the firm's pace suggests demand remains for structured solutions even as senior construction lenders pull back.

What remains unknown is whether the near-$1 billion figure represents closed transactions, committed capital, or total deal volume including pipeline activity. The dossier does not specify the firm's fee structure, the timing of each placement, or the identity of the capital sources behind the deals. Future reporting should watch whether WD Capital discloses a full transaction list, whether the Oasis Hallandale East Tower financing closes as described, and whether the firm's mix of mezzanine and construction placements continues as senior lending conditions evolve.
