Rising 17 stories above the Myrtle-Wyckoff subway hub where Ridgewood meets Bushwick, the glass-and-masonry tower at 3-50 St. Nicholas Avenue (recorded under lead parcel 16-37 Woodbine Street) is the tallest private landmark for miles. On September 8, 2026, CDK 3-50 St. Nicholas LLC closed a $75.83 million buyout of the four-parcel mixed-use tower from Ridgewood Tower LLC, marking a 138.3 percent valuation step-up over the $31.82 million basis recorded five years earlier in September 2021.

Measure the deal by replacement cost and the logic snaps into focus. The $75.83 million purchase equates to $382 a square foot (or $574,437 per apartment across 128 residential units) across 198,500 square feet, climbing 138.3 percent above the $31.82 million price tag recorded in September 2021 while still clearing below what it costs to assemble, carry, and build comparable Ridgewood product today. Based on underwritten net operating income, the trade clears at a 6.00 percent going-in cap rate.

How a 17-Story Myrtle-Wyckoff Hub Tower Cleared $75.8 Million

Rather than layering new acquisition debt onto the four-parcel transfer at closing, CDK 3-50 St. Nicholas LLC recorded the $75.83 million deed transfer without a simultaneous new mortgage—consolidating ownership of the 2022-built tower at $382 per gross square foot.

Sitting directly atop the L and M subway transfer station and a multi-line bus terminal, the 17-story C4-3 tower combines multi-level national credit retail (including Burlington and healthcare tenants) with panoramic upper-floor apartments—generating an underwritten 6.00 percent cap rate that institutional buyers struggle to find in Manhattan.

From Our Desk

Looking at 16-37 Woodbine Street from our debt and equity desk at Light Tower Group, one takeaway stands out for sponsors sizing up Ridgewood. Anchoring ground-floor national retail and medical office beneath 17 stories of transit-oriented apartments right above the L and M trains created an outer-borough cash machine that more than doubled its 2021 valuation.
