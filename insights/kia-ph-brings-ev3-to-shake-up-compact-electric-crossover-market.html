Kia PH Brings EV3 to Shake Up Compact Electric Crossover Market

Kia Philippines launched the EV3 on September 21 at P1.898 million, positioning the subcompact electric crossover against the BYD Atto 3 and incoming Suzuki e V

Ben Rohr September 28, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Kia Philippines expanded its local electric lineup with the September 21 rollout of the EV3, pricing the subcompact model at P1.898 million (roughly $30,400) to take aim directly at rival battery-powered options. The Ayala-backed distributor introduced the nameplate in a solitary GL trim, slotted beneath the larger EV5 and EV9 flagships. Early buyers receive a promotional 30,000-peso reduction along with a complimentary home wall-box charger and five years of free scheduled maintenance.

Built on a dedicated 400-volt configuration of the Electric-Global Modular Platform, the EV3 employs nickel-manganese-cobalt chemistry. In the Philippine market, it utilizes a 58.3-kilowatt-hour battery yielding up to 511 kilometers of driving distance under NEDC testing, translating to roughly 430 kilometers on the WLTP cycle. Direct-current fast charging achieves a 10 to 80 percent benchmark in 29 minutes, while an 11-kilowatt alternating-current connection takes five hours and 20 minutes. Propulsion flows exclusively to the front axle through a permanent magnet synchronous motor producing 204 horsepower and 283 newton-meters of pulling force, yielding a zero-to-100 km/h acceleration run in 7.5 seconds.

Industry watchers note the entry occupies a fierce niche, competing squarely against China's BYD Atto 3 while undercutting incoming Japanese contenders like Suzuki's e Vitara. Kia also relies heavily on ACMobility's accelerating national charging infrastructure rollout to reassure prospective adopters. Ownership coverage bundles a five-year, 160,000-kilometer general warranty alongside eight years of protection for the high-voltage battery, which may strengthen the value proposition against lower-priced Chinese rivals.

What remains unknown is whether the solitary GL trim and 58.3-kWh battery will satisfy Philippine buyers who might expect the larger 81.4-kWh Long Range variant available in global markets. The dossier does not specify local sales targets, reservation volumes, or how the promotional pricing window compares with BYD's actual transaction prices. The real-world range under Philippine driving conditions and the pace of ACMobility's charger deployment will likely determine whether the EV3 can convert competitive positioning into sustained market share.
