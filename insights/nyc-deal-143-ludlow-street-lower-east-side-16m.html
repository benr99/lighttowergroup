Lower East Side Walk-Up at 143 Ludlow Street Fetches $16.39M All-Cash ($1,201/SF)

Writing an unencumbered $16.39 million check, 143 Ludlow LLC takes control of 13,650 square feet at $1,201 a square foot.

Between Stanton and Rivington streets in the heart of the Lower East Side's nightlife and boutique hotel corridor, a renovated six-story brick walk-up with 23 free-market apartments and two bustling storefronts just traded for more than double its 2012 price. 143 Ludlow LLC wired $16.39 million all-cash on August 25, 2026 to acquire 143 Ludlow Street from Ludlow Homa, LLC, pricing the 13,650-square-foot property at $1,201 per square foot ($712,522 per residential unit) and marking a 134.1 percent gain over the $7 million sale recorded in July 2012.

Measure the deal by replacement cost and the logic snaps into focus. The $16.39 million purchase equates to $1,201 a square foot (or $655,520 per apartment across 23 residential units) across 13,650 square feet, climbing 134.1 percent above the $7 million price tag recorded in July 2012 while still clearing below what it costs to assemble, carry, and build comparable Lower East Side product today. Based on underwritten net operating income, the trade clears at a 6.00 percent going-in cap rate.

All-Cash at $1,201 a Foot on the Lower East Side

While rent-stabilized pre-war buildings across the five boroughs have traded at steep discounts since 2019, 100 percent free-market downtown walk-ups sit at the opposite end of the spectrum. 143 Ludlow LLC closed the entire $16.39 million purchase without a dollar of recorded mortgage debt.

On Ludlow Street—half a block from Soho House's Ludlow House and the Essex Market F/J/M/Z subway hub—young professionals happily pay top-of-market rents for renovated walk-up residences above destination restaurant retail, handing the all-cash buyer a clean 6.00 percent unlevered yield.
