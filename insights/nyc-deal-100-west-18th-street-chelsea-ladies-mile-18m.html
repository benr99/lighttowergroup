At the southwest corner of Sixth Avenue and West 18th Street in Chelsea's historic Ladies' Mile district, wraparound retail frontage catches seven-day foot traffic between Flatiron and the High Line. On August 25, 2026, 611 AOFA LLC closed on the three-unit commercial and retail assemblage at 100 West 18th Street (also known as 611 Sixth Avenue) for $18.3 million ($2,313 per square foot), purchasing the asset from The Retail Stores of 611 6th Ave., LLC.

Across the property's 7,912 square feet on a 3,956-square-foot C6-2A lot, the $18.3 million trade establishes a $2,313 a square foot clearing mark for Chelsea / Ladies' Mile Prime Corner Retail Condominium & Commercial Assemblage product. Based on underwritten net operating income, the trade clears at a 6.20 percent going-in cap rate.

Peapack Private Bank Delivers a $12.75 Million CEMA at 69.7 Percent LTV

To finance the $2,313-per-square-foot acquisition, New Jersey-based Peapack Private Bank & Trust stepped in with a $12.75 million senior loan—representing 69.7 percent loan-to-value—leaving 611 AOFA LLC to write a $5.55 million equity check.

Structuring the $12.75 million loan via an ACRIS AGMT consolidation (CEMA) saved the buyers roughly $357,000 in mortgage recording taxes at closing. Combined with a 6.20 percent going-in cap rate from the corner's Sixth Avenue retail tenants, Peapack's 69.7 percent leverage gives the sponsorship immediate positive leverage.
