Thompson Thrift Lands $463M Bridge Loan for 8 Rentals

JLL arranged $463 million in bridge financing for Thompson Thrift’s eight-property multifamily portfolio, six of which are in Florida.

Ben Rohr October 14, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

JLL’s Capital Markets group arranged $463 million in bridge financing for Thompson Thrift’s eight-property multifamily portfolio. 6 of the 8 properties are in Florida. That’s a lot of new apartments in one state.

JLL secured the floating-rate construction take-out loan through Barings, with Daniel Hedgepeth leading the financing. The proceeds retired existing construction loans across all eight properties, which were delivered on a phased basis throughout 2025 and 2026. I read this as a developer swapping eight construction loans for one bridge loan.

The floating rate is the part I keep coming back to.
