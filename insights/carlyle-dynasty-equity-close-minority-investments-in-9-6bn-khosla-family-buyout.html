Carlyle, Dynasty Equity close minority investments in $9.6bn Khosla family buyout of Seattle Seahawks

Carlyle and sports-focused private equity firm Dynasty Equity were among minority investments in the Seattle Seahawks, supporting the Khosla family’s $9.6bn acquisition of the NFL franchise. The transaction was reported by AltAssets Private Equity News on September 4, 2026, and describes the Seahawks as current Super Bowl holders. The deal is categorized as a buyout, with the minority investments closing alongside the Khosla family’s controlling acquisition.

The report names Carlyle and Dynasty Equity as minority investors, while the headline also references Sixth Street. However, the available summary text does not specify Sixth Street’s role, the size of any individual minority stake, or the financing structure behind the $9.6bn valuation. The source is a single secondary feed summary from AltAssets, and the full article is behind a subscription paywall, limiting the accessible detail to the headline and a brief summary paragraph.

The participation of established private equity firms in an NFL franchise acquisition is consistent with the league’s evolving approach to institutional ownership, but this single summary does not provide enough evidence to assess broader market trends, seller identity, or the Khosla family’s prior sports holdings. The reported $9.6bn figure appears to represent the total acquisition value, though the dossier does not clarify whether that reflects enterprise value, equity value, or a combination of debt and equity.

What remains unknown includes the exact ownership percentages acquired by Carlyle, Dynasty Equity, and Sixth Street; the identity of the selling party; any league approval conditions; and the timing of the transaction’s completion. Because the evidence level is a single summary with no primary documentation, the article can state that the minority investments closed, but cannot independently verify the deal’s terms or the accuracy of the $9.6bn valuation beyond the source’s reporting.
