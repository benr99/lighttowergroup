Williamsburg Bridge Plaza Portfolio at 277 South 5th Street Sells for $20.5M With $20M Structured Note

With Levon NY Dime LLC supplying $20 million in acquisition financing, Dime Commercial Holdings LLC locks in 277 South 5th Street at $915 a square foot.

At the foot of the Williamsburg Bridge plaza where Havemeyer Street meets South 5th Street, corner commercial and residential buildings sit at the literal gateway to Brooklyn. Dime Commercial Holdings LLC paid $20.5 million ($915 per square foot across the three-parcel portfolio) on August 24, 2026 to acquire 277 South 5th Street and its companion Havemeyer lots from Havemeyer Owner LLC, backed by a $20 million structured mortgage from Levon NY Dime LLC.

Run the numbers across 22,400 square feet and the buyer's thesis comes into view. At $915 a square foot (or $854,167 per apartment across 18 residential units) on a 5,400-square-foot parcel zoned C4-3, Dime Commercial Holdings LLC is locking in irreplaceable Williamsburg brick well below new-construction replacement cost.

High-Octane Structured Credit on the Williamsburg Bridge Plaza

The ACRIS mortgage ledger shows Levon NY Dime LLC advancing $20 million against the $20.5 million deed transfer—a 97.6 percent loan-to-purchase-price attachment point that signals either a cross-collateralized facility or a structured note purchase converted into fee title.

Sitting directly across from the Marcy Avenue J/M/Z subway station and the Williamsburg Bridge bus terminal under C4-3 commercial zoning, the three-parcel Havemeyer and South 5th Street corner commands relentless commuter foot traffic and prime signage visibility.

From Our Desk

Looking at 277 South 5th Street from our debt and equity desk at Light Tower Group, one takeaway stands out for sponsors sizing up Williamsburg. Recording a $20 million private credit facility against a $20.5 million purchase price at the Marcy/Havemeyer transit hub points to a cross-collateralized or heavy value-add execution where the lender is underwriting the stabilized rent roll after repositioning.
