ACORE Capital Provides $81M Financing for Philadelphia Multifamily Portfolio

ACORE Capital has provided $81 million in financing for a multifamily portfolio in Philadelphia's Olde Kensington neighborhood, refinancing two built-to-rent to

Ben Rohr October 04, 2026 2 min read 1 source

ACORE Capital, a U.S. commercial real estate investment manager, announced it has provided $81 million in financing for a multifamily portfolio in Philadelphia's Olde Kensington neighborhood. The transaction matters because it combines refinancing and construction funding under a single facility, a structure that reflects how lenders are adapting to borrowers who need capital across different stages of an asset's life cycle. The deal also signals continued institutional appetite for rental housing in Philadelphia, a market the lender describes as having durable demand for high-quality rental housing.

The financing is structured to accomplish two distinct objectives. It refinances two existing built-to-rent townhouse assets and funds construction of a third traditional mixed-use multifamily property. Together, the properties include approximately 234 residences across three modern residential communities. The portfolio has convenient access to major employment corridors, public transportation, and the city's retail, dining, and cultural destinations. Construction of the 120-unit mixed-use multifamily property is underway, with delivery anticipated in Q3 2028. Roughly half of the proceeds will be used toward construction, according to the announcement.

The evidence for this transaction comes from a single full-text source: a Yield PRO article published on October 3, 2026. The article quotes Corey Goodstein, a Director within the Originations team at ACORE Capital, who said the transaction reflects the firm's ability to use creative capital solutions to meet borrower needs by providing bridge and construction financing under one facility. The financing was arranged by Jason Hull, Managing Director at Regions Bank. ACORE Capital is described as a leading U.S. commercial real estate investment manager with more than $17 billion of assets under management as of June 30, 2026, headquartered in New York with offices in Los Angeles, Miami, San Francisco, and Dallas.

The deal has implications for the commercial real estate lending sector, particularly in the multifamily space. By combining bridge refinancing for stabilized built-to-rent assets with construction financing for a new mixed-use project, ACORE Capital is positioning itself as a flexible capital provider capable of handling complex, multi-asset portfolios. The focus on built-to-rent townhouses also highlights the growing institutional interest in single-family rental and townhouse rental formats, which have gained traction as alternatives to traditional apartment buildings. The Philadelphia location, with its access to employment corridors and transit, suggests lenders are prioritizing well-located assets in markets with demonstrated rental demand.

Several limitations and unknowns remain. The announcement does not disclose the borrower's identity, the interest rate, loan term, loan-to-value ratio, or other material financing terms. It also does not specify the exact breakdown of proceeds between the refinancing and construction components beyond the statement that roughly half will go toward construction. The source is a secondary trade publication, and no corroborating announcement from ACORE Capital or Regions Bank is included in the available evidence. The $17 billion assets under management figure is dated as of June 30, 2026, which may not reflect the firm's current position. Future reporting should watch for confirmation of the borrower, final construction timelines, and whether the mixed-use property leases as anticipated upon delivery in Q3 2028.
