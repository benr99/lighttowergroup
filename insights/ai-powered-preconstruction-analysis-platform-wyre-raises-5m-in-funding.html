AI-Powered Preconstruction Analysis Platform Wyre Raises $5M in Funding

Wyre, an AI-powered document intelligence platform founded by Pype creator Sunil Dorairajan, announced $5 million in pre-seed and seed funding on Sept. 10.

Ben Rohr September 13, 2026 2 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Wyre, an AI-powered document intelligence platform that scans construction drawings and specifications to produce structured scopes, risk insights, and traceable references for contractors, announced $5 million in pre-seed and seed funding on Sept. 10. The company was founded by Sunil Dorairajan, who previously founded Pype and sold it to Autodesk in 2020. Wyre offers two products: Wyre Scopes, which uses machine learning to analyze document sets and deliver traceable scope packages, and Wyre Check, an AI-powered service that checks documents and resulting shop drawings for clashes.

The seed round was led by Ironspring Ventures, with participation from WND Ventures, the corporate venture arm of DPR Construction, and Virginia Innovation Partnership Corporation. Angel investors included Motif CEO Amar Hanspal, a former Autodesk co-CEO; Rajesh Ram, co-founder and chief growth officer at Egnyte; and Joseph Khoury, vice president of Preconstruction and co-founder of MCN Build. Wyre also brought in Varada Pyda as chief technology officer. Pyda was previously head of engineering at Pype and most recently led AI solutions at Autodesk Construction Solutions before rejoining Dorairajan.

The funding supports Wyre's effort to compete in an increasingly crowded field of document review AI, where it faces industry players such as Procore's DataGrid, Trunk Tools, and Pype, now at Autodesk. Dorairajan said the company is building its intellectual property layer on trained model data and information tagged by subject matter experts who validated scope information. Ty Findley, co-founder and general partner at Ironspring Ventures, said preconstruction is "the phase where the highest-stakes decisions are made and margins are set," and cited that challenge as a reason for leading the round. Wyre is already being tested by roughly a dozen contractor customers in various stages of deployment, according to the announcement.

What remains unknown from the single source is the split between pre-seed and seed funding, the valuation, and any specific revenue or customer growth figures. The announcement also does not detail how Wyre's subject matter expert validation process compares in cost or speed to competitors, or whether the roughly dozen contractor tests have converted to paid contracts. The source is a secondary trade publication, and no independent confirmation of the funding terms or customer claims was available in the dossier.
