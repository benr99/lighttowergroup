JLL arranges $456m for Family Dollar’s national distribution center portfolio

JLL Capital Markets announced a $455.7 million refinancing for an eight-property, 7.1 million-square-foot distribution center portfolio fully leased to Family D

Ben Rohr September 24, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

JLL Capital Markets announced that it has arranged a $455.7 million refinancing for an eight-property, 7.1 million-square-foot national distribution center portfolio leased to Family Dollar. The portfolio consists of eight bulk distribution centers totaling 7,106,000 square feet and is 100 percent leased to Family Dollar under a long-term absolute triple-net master lease. The facilities were built between 1997 and 2013 and range from 832,000 to 907,000 square feet, with clear heights from 30 to 42 feet.

JLL worked on behalf of the borrower, 1959 RE Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company controlled and partially owned by affiliates of Brigade Capital Management and Macellum Capital Management. Wells Fargo served as sole lead on the floating-rate refinancing. The portfolio benefits from broad geographic diversification and access to major transportation corridors, according to the announcement.

The refinancing suggests continued lender appetite for industrial assets backed by long-term, triple-net leases to a single national tenant. The floating-rate structure and sole-lead arrangement indicate a concentrated financing execution, though the announcement does not disclose loan term, spread, or use of proceeds. The 100 percent occupancy under a master lease reduces tenant rollover risk but concentrates credit exposure in Family Dollar.

What remains unknown is the loan's maturity, interest rate details, and any conditions tied to the master lease. The announcement also does not specify whether the refinancing replaces existing debt or provides additional proceeds to the borrower. Because the evidence comes from a single summary, the transaction's broader market implications cannot be independently assessed.
