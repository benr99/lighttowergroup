Brixmor and Everview Agree to Acquire Slate Grocery REIT for $2.3 Billion

Two New York City-based firms, Brixmor Property Group and Everview Partners, have agreed to acquire Toronto-based Slate Grocery REIT for approximately $2.3 bill

Ben Rohr September 28, 2026 2 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Brixmor Property Group and Everview Partners have entered into an agreement to acquire Toronto-based Slate Grocery REIT for approximately $2.3 billion. The transaction matters because it gives Brixmor, a retail owner-operator with a national portfolio of 346 retail centers totaling roughly 63 million square feet, direct ownership of 23 grocery-anchored shopping centers while also positioning the company as asset manager, property manager, and leasing representative for a much larger joint venture portfolio. The deal is expected to close in the first quarter of next year, according to the announcement.

The transaction is structured in two parts. Brixmor will acquire a portfolio of 23 grocery-anchored shopping centers totaling roughly 3 million square feet for $636 million. Those properties are concentrated predominantly across Florida, Georgia, and the Carolinas, with an aggregate occupancy rate of about 96 percent and anchors including Publix, Kroger, and Harris Teeter. Brixmor will take full ownership of 22 of the centers and 50 percent ownership of a single center. Separately, a newly formed joint venture between Brixmor and affiliates of Everview Partners will acquire Slate Grocery's remaining 92 properties totaling approximately 12 million square feet for approximately $1.7 billion. Within that joint venture, Brixmor will hold a 20 percent common equity interest and Everview will hold an 80 percent common equity interest.

The only source for this transaction is a Shopping Center Business article published on September 28, 2026, which reports the announcement without additional corroborating filings or disclosures. The article quotes Brixmor CEO and president Brian Finnegan, who said, "Across both the wholly owned and joint venture assets, we see meaningful embedded value through below-market rents and a robust pipeline of remerchandising, redevelopment and outparcel opportunities." Finnegan added that Brixmor's "extensive retailer relationships and proven execution capabilities" are expected to unlock that value and generate cash flow growth over time. The article also notes that a wholly owned subsidiary of the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority will act as a strategic investor alongside Everview in the transaction. Brixmor's stock price opened at $28.15 per share on Monday, Sept. 28, the day the transaction was announced, relatively unchanged from $27.50 per share a year ago.

The sector implications are notable for grocery-anchored retail. Brixmor has identified approximately $100 million of redevelopment and outparcel development opportunities within the 23 wholly owned centers, including several potential Publix redevelopment projects. The 96 percent occupancy rate on those properties suggests stable cash flow, while the emphasis on below-market rents and remerchandising indicates a value-add strategy rather than a purely stabilized acquisition. The joint venture structure also allows Brixmor to expand its management footprint across 92 additional properties without deploying capital for full ownership, while Everview and the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority subsidiary provide the majority of equity for that portion.

The evidence base is limited to a single full-text source, so several material details remain unconfirmed. The article does not specify the per-share consideration for Slate Grocery REIT shareholders, the financing structure, any debt assumptions, or the expected closing conditions beyond the first-quarter timing. It also does not provide historical financials for Slate Grocery or a breakdown of the joint venture portfolio's occupancy, tenant mix, or geographic concentration. Investors should watch for subsequent regulatory filings or company disclosures that confirm the transaction terms, the role of the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority subsidiary, and any changes to Brixmor's leverage or earnings guidance resulting from the acquisition.
