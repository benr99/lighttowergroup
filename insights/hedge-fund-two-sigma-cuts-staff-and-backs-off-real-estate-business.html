Hedge Fund Two Sigma Cuts Staff And Backs Off Real Estate Business

The private quantitative hedge fund launched fundraising for its real estate investments in 2024 with its Real Estate Opportunity Fund.

Ben Rohr October 20, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

That number says more than any press release.

The private quantitative hedge fund launched fundraising for its real estate investments in 2024 with its Real Estate Opportunity Fund. The vehicle ultimately pulled in less than half its $2B target.

Two of the team’s three partners are headed for the exit along with one of the two heads of data science.

Two Sigma started investing in a real estate portfolio in 2019 before formally launching the vertical two years later with internal capital. It opened to outside cash in 2024, pledging to focus on housing and industrial assets with selective investments in other sectors.

Private fundraising for commercial real estate is lagging in 2026. The asset class attracted $30B in the three months that ended in September, the lowest quarterly total since 2013, according to data from PEI Group.

Several other real estate investment vehicles have been rolled up this year as the financing backdrop becomes increasingly challenged. German asset management giant DWS Group announced the planned liquidation of RREEF Property Trust in September amid heightened redemption requests. Apollo Global Management said it would liquidate its publicly traded REIT focused on commercial real estate, which had already been shedding assets, after management completed a review of strategic alternatives.

JPMorgan Asset Management announced in May that it would liquidate its JP Morgan US Real Estate Income and Growth Fund.

Easy to announce, harder to underwrite.
