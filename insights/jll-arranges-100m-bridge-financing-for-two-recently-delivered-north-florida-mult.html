JLL Arranges $100M Bridge Financing for Two Recently Delivered North Florida Multifamily Assets

JLL Capital Markets has arranged $100 million in floating-rate bridge financing for Waypoint Residential's two-property portfolio in Jacksonville and St. August

Ben Rohr October 06, 2026 3 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

JLL Capital Markets announced that it has arranged $100 million in bridge financing for Waypoint Residential's two-property multifamily portfolio in northern Florida, facilitating the recapitalization of recently completed construction projects. The transaction matters because it shows how well-capitalized borrowers are using floating-rate construction take-out financing to move newly delivered assets from construction debt into a flexible bridge structure, preserving optionality to sell once stabilization is achieved. JLL worked on behalf of the borrower, Waypoint Residential, to secure the financing with TPG Real Estate Credit.

The portfolio consists of The Pointe at Davis Creek, a 288-unit garden-style community at 12175 Philips Highway in Jacksonville, and The Bradley St. Augustine, a 250-unit garden-style community at 350 Bay Laurel Drive in St. Augustine. Both properties delivered in Q1 2025 and feature resort-style pools with tanning ledges and cabanas, 24-hour fitness centers with yoga and spin studios, pet spas, outdoor gathering areas with fire pits and grilling stations, resident lounges with coffee bars and co-working spaces, and on-site leasing and management offices. Unit interiors include walk-in closets, gourmet kitchens with granite countertops and islands, luxury vinyl plank flooring, energy-efficient stainless-steel appliances, full-size washers and dryers, and private patios or balconies.

The evidence comes from a single full-text source, Yield PRO, published on October 5, 2026. The announcement quotes JLL's Executive Managing Director Matthew Lawton, who said the transaction "demonstrates the strength of Waypoint's vertically integrated platform and TPG's continued confidence in high-quality Sun Belt multifamily fundamentals." Lawton added that both properties are "performing exceptionally well in their respective lease-up trajectories" and that the flexible bridge financing structure positions Waypoint to execute its stabilization business plan while maintaining optionality to sell once the assets reach maturity. Senior Managing Director Travis Anderson noted that Jacksonville and St. Augustine rank among the fastest-growing markets in the Southeast, with population within one mile of the properties growing over 4% annually on average over the past five years, and average household incomes exceeding $104,000 within the immediate trade areas.

The sector implications are tied to Sun Belt multifamily fundamentals and the mechanics of construction take-out lending. The Pointe at Davis Creek is positioned just off Interstate 95 near the intersection with State Road 202, with proximity to St. Johns Town Center, a 1.5 million-square-foot super-regional outdoor mall recently renovated by Simon Property Group, and The Avenues, a 1.5 million-square-foot regional mall. The Bradley St. Augustine is located in the southern corridor of St. Augustine, approximately 5.6 miles from historic downtown St. Augustine, surrounded by golf communities including St. Johns Golf Club and St. Augustine Shores Golf Club, with connectivity via Interstate 95 and State Road A1A to Jacksonville, the beaches, and regional employment centers. The floating-rate structure suggests the borrower is prioritizing flexibility over long-term fixed-rate certainty during the lease-up and stabilization period.

The source provides no information on loan term, spread, interest rate cap requirements, loan-to-cost or loan-to-value ratios, recourse provisions, or the specific use of proceeds beyond recapitalization. It also does not disclose current occupancy, lease-up pace, or rent levels at either property. JLL's Capital Markets Debt Advisory team representing the borrower was led by Executive Managing Director Matthew Lawton, Senior Managing Directors Travis Anderson, Melissa Quinn, Gregg Shapiro and Dan Kearns, with support from Vice President Kelsey Bawcombe, Analyst Streeter Simmons and Vice President Rob Rothaug. JLL is a Fortune 500 company with annual revenue of $26.1 billion, operations in over 80 countries, and a global workforce of more than 112,000 as of June 30, 2026. What to watch is whether Waypoint can complete stabilization and whether the optionality to sell is exercised, which would provide a market signal on pricing for recently delivered Sun Belt multifamily assets.
