Inside the Hunts Point peninsula—the food and freight distribution engine that feeds 22 million metro-area residents—warehouses with 10,000 square feet of extra yard space next to the Bruckner Expressway rarely leave family hands. Jacobs 1260, LLC closed on the 50,000-square-foot warehouse at 1265 Oak Point Avenue on August 20, 2026 for $21.6 million ($432 per building square foot on a 60,000-square-foot lot), acquiring the property from Oak Point Bergson Realty Corp.

Every Bronx submarket has a number where institutional buyers stop watching from the sidelines and start wiring earnest money. In Hunts Point, that clearing level is $432 a square foot across 50,000 square feet under M1-2 zoning.

Tapping the NYC Industrial Development Agency for $10.7 Million

Look at the lender recorded on ACRIS document 2026082600398004: the New York City Industrial Development Agency (NYCIDA), which provided $10.7 million in financing—representing 49.5 percent of the $21.6 million purchase price—alongside $10.9 million of sponsor equity.

For an industrial user-buyer or cold-storage distributor in Hunts Point, qualifying for an NYCIDA transaction unlocks a triple crown of municipal incentives: mortgage recording tax exemptions, long-term real estate tax abatements (PILOT), and sales-tax exemptions on warehouse modernization materials.

From Our Desk

For sponsors watching the Bronx tape from our desk at Light Tower Group, Jacobs 1260, LLC's move at 1265 Oak Point Avenue sends a clear signal. Pairing a $21.6 million Hunts Point warehouse purchase with $10.7 million in New York City Industrial Development Agency financing at 49.5 percent LTV delivers municipal mortgage-recording-tax exemptions and PILOT incentives that supercharge industrial cash-on-cash returns.
