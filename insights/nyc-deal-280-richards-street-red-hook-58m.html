KKR Real Estate Partners Americas & Alpha Industrial Properties Shells Out $58M for Red Hook's 280 Richards Street With PGIM Real Estate Financing

In a market where bid-ask spreads have kept dozens of New York City investment sales on ice, KKR Real Estate Partners Americas & Alpha Industrial Properties via KKR Red Hook Logistics Owner LLC stepped in to close on 280 Richards Street in Red Hook, Brooklyn for $58,000,000 ($58M). The fee-simple deed transfer, which closed on 2026-03-20 and hit the city register on 2026-03-27, prices the 312,000-square-foot class a urban logistics warehouse at $186/GSF, marking a 39.8% step-up over the $41,500,000 basis recorded in 2019 (6.4-year hold).

The Deal & The Players

KKR Real Estate Partners Americas & Alpha Industrial Properties via KKR Red Hook Logistics Owner LLC acquired the 100% interest from Thor Equities (Joe Sitt) via Thor 280 Richards Street LLC, with Cushman & Wakefield (capital markets investment sales and debt advisory) advising on the trade. ACRIS Document ID 2026032700619001 records the $58,000,000 sale of 280 Richards Street (BBL 3-00597-0001) in Red Hook, Brooklyn from Thor 280 Richards Street LLC (Thor Equities, led by Joe Sitt) to KKR Red Hook Logistics Owner LLC (KKR Real Estate and Alpha Industrial Properties). PGIM Real Estate provided a $37,700,000 senior mortgage (ACRIS Document ID 2026032700619002) at 65% LTV alongside $20,300,000 of implied equity on the 312,000-square-foot, 2-story M3-1 Class A warehouse completed in 2022. The trade establishes a $186-per-gross-square-foot basis for modern 32-foot-clear Red Hook last-mile logistics with 41 loading docks and direct access to Lower Manhattan via the Hugh L. Carey Tunnel, marking a 39.8% step-up over the $41,500,000 basis recorded in November 2019.

The Capital Stack X-Ray

To fund the acquisition, the buyer locked in a $37,700,000 senior mortgage (ACRIS Document ID 2026032700619002) from PGIM Real Estate, representing an implied loan-to-value ratio of 65% alongside $20,300,000 of sponsor equity. Sitting behind the senior note is $20,300,000 in implied equity ($20.3M), illustrating the disciplined 60% to 70% senior attachment points that are actually clearing New York City credit committees in 2026.

The Asset & Street Reality

Identified on the city tax map as BBL 3-00597-0001 under building class E9 (Class A Urban Logistics Warehouse), 280 Richards Street spans 312,000 gross square feet across 2 stories on a 195,000-square-foot lot zoned M3-1 (built 2022).

The Desk Read

On our placement desk at Light Tower Group, 280 Richards Street is a textbook readout of how institutional capital is pricing New York City risk right now. First, clearing basis is everything: at $186/GSF, the sponsorship is buying Red Hook footage at a basis that insulates the business plan against rate volatility. Second, look at the debt execution: PGIM Real Estate stepping up for $37.7M at 65% LTV proves that credit committees are wide open for right-sized basis and institutional sponsorship—especially when borrowers use CEMA structures or agency/life-company execution to protect day-one cash yield.
