AEW Sells Industrial Park For $61M: The Los Angeles Deal Sheet

Ben Rohr September 28, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

AEW sold the Walnut Tech Business Center, a 200K SF shallow-bay business park in Walnut, for $60.7M to CIP Real Estate II LLC, according to a Bisnow report. The JLL Capital Markets team of Patrick Nally, Evan Moran, Chad Solomon, Shae Vomund and Lauren O'Neill represented seller AEW. A separate JLL team of Kevin Mackenzie, Peter Thompson, Kyle White and Colter Smith arranged acquisition financing through an unnamed bank on behalf of the buyer.

The transaction was one of several industrial and commercial deals in the Los Angeles area covered in the same report. Oxford Properties sold a four-building, 238K SF light industrial portfolio in Riverside to Sierra Ridge Capital for an undisclosed price, with JLL's Jeff Sause, Danny Ryan and Jenny Barger arranging a $25M acquisition loan. Lincoln Property Co. acquired Bellegrave Business Park, a six-building, 327K SF industrial park in Jurupa Valley, on behalf of a separate account client, with financial details undisclosed.

The Walnut sale adds to evidence of continued investor interest in Southern California shallow-bay industrial assets, a segment that has drawn attention for its role in last-mile logistics and small-business occupancy. The involvement of JLL on both the sale and financing sides suggests institutional-grade execution for a mid-sized industrial park, though the report does not disclose the buyer's financing terms or the seller's investment horizon.

What remains unknown is the capitalization rate, the buyer's business plan, and whether the $60.7M price reflects a premium or discount to recent comparable trades. The dossier relies on a single secondary source, Bisnow, and does not include independent confirmation of the sale price or financing details. The unnamed bank and undisclosed loan terms limit any assessment of leverage or return expectations for CIP Real Estate II LLC.
