From a $2.5M Shell to an $11.4M Boerum Hill Trophy: 45 Douglass Street Clears $2,463 a Foot

Backed by a $6 million loan from Morgan Stanley Private Bank, N.A., the Boerum Hill closing clears at $2,463 a square foot across 4,626 square feet.

Four years ago, 45 Douglass Street was a $2.5 million gut-renovation shell on a tree-lined Boerum Hill block between Smith and Court streets; this month, it set a neighborhood benchmark north of $2,460 a square foot. Lands 45 LLC closed on the newly transformed, 25-foot-wide fee-simple townhouse at 45 Douglass Street on September 8, 2026 for $11.4 million, delivering a 355.8 percent gross step-up over the $2.5 million shell price paid by 45 Douglass Holdings, LLC in March 2022.

The per-foot math explains why 45 Douglass Holdings, LLC chose this moment to harvest gains—and why Lands 45 LLC was willing to pay up. At $2,463 a square foot across 4,626 square feet, the closing price represents a 355.8 percent appreciation over the $2.5 million trade recorded in March 2022.

Morgan Stanley Private Bank Writes $6 Million at 52.7 Percent LTV

To finance the $11.4 million purchase, Lands 45 LLC secured a $6 million senior mortgage from Morgan Stanley Private Bank, N.A. (52.7 percent loan-to-value), pairing private-bank balance-sheet pricing with $5.4 million in buyer equity.

For boutique Brooklyn value-add developers, 45 Douglass Street is the ultimate proof of concept: acquiring an extra-wide 25-by-100-foot R6B lot for $2.5 million in 2022, executing an architectural gut reconstruction across 4,626 square feet, and exiting into the private-wealth townhouse market above $11 million.

From Our Desk

On our placement desk at Light Tower Group, 45 Douglass Street is a live case study in how Brooklyn deals actually cross the finish line right now. Ultra-wide brownstone redevelopments in Boerum Hill and Cobble Hill remain one of the few NYC residential strategies where value-add sponsors can turn a $2.5 million shell into an $11.4 million private-bank-financed exit inside of four and a half years.
