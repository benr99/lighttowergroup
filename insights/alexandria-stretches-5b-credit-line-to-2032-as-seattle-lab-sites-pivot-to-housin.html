Alexandria Stretches $5B Credit Line to 2032 as Seattle Lab Sites Pivot to Housing and Tech

Ben Rohr September 29, 2026 1 min read 1 source

Alexandria Real Estate Equities extended its $5 billion revolving credit facility to 2032 at a lower spread, according to a report from The Registry Puget Sound. The refinancing is described as buying runway for the REIT's $2.9 billion sale program while it repositions Seattle lab holdings toward apartments and advanced technology tenants. The report is a single feed summary, and the full article sits behind a subscription wall, limiting the detail available on pricing, covenants, or lender composition.

The same source feed lists two additional Puget Sound transactions from September 29, 2026, though they are not tied to Alexandria in the available text. Infill Industrial Partners paid $31.8 million for a 126,457-square-foot Renton warehouse, and a Dallas-based buyer paid $10.5 million for the former Rexam beverage can site. These figures appear as adjacent market activity in the feed rather than as components of Alexandria's repositioning or sale program.

The supported implication is narrow: Alexandria is using a longer-dated, lower-spread credit facility to create time and flexibility for asset sales and a strategic shift in Seattle. The pivot toward housing and advanced technology tenants suggests the company sees stronger demand outside traditional lab use in that market, but the dossier does not provide occupancy data, cap rates, buyer identities, or the proportion of the $2.9 billion program tied to Seattle.

What remains unknown is substantial. The dossier does not specify the previous maturity date, the new spread, the size of the lender group, or whether the facility is secured or unsecured. It also does not identify which Seattle lab sites are being converted, the expected unit counts for apartments, or the advanced technology tenants being targeted. Because the evidence level is a single summary, no broader thesis about Seattle lab demand or Alexandria's balance sheet should be advanced beyond the stated refinancing and repositioning facts.
