S3 Capital Backs $15.6M Purchase of Williamsburg Development Site at 60 North First Street

Backed by a $13.3 million loan from S3 Capital, the Williamsburg closing clears at $520 a square foot across 30,000 square feet.

One block inland from Domino Park on Williamsburg's Northside, a clean 10,000-square-foot through-lot zoned M1-2/R6A is the kind of boutique development canvas that draws bids from every condo builder in Brooklyn. On September 9, 2026, 60 North 1st LLC closed on 60 North First Street for $15.6 million from AG Realty LLC, pricing the 30,000-buildable-square-foot Northside site at $520 per buildable foot.

Across the property's 30,000 square feet on a 10,000-square-foot M1-2/R6A lot, the $15.6 million trade establishes a $520 a square foot clearing mark for Williamsburg Mixed-Use Waterfront Development Site product.

S3 Capital Supplies $13.3 Million at 85 Percent of Purchase Price

To finance the land takedown, the buyer turned to Joshua Crane and Robert Schwartz's S3 Capital (via S3 RE 60 N1 Funding LLC), which recorded a $13.3 million senior mortgage alongside the deed—representing an eye-catching 85.3 percent loan-to-purchase-price ratio.

Why would a disciplined private lender advance 85.3 percent on a Williamsburg development site? Because in North Williamsburg between Kent and Wythe avenues, boutique luxury condominiums routinely sell out between $2,100 and $2,500 per square foot—making a $520-per-buildable-foot land basis deeply defensive when paired with shovel-ready architectural plans.
