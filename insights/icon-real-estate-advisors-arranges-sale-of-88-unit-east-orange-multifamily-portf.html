Icon Real Estate Advisors Arranges Sale of 88-Unit East Orange Multifamily Portfolio

A lender-controlled REO sale of two East Orange properties gives MNM Holding LLC an 88-unit renovation and lease-up opportunity, while pushing ICON's year-to-da

Ben Rohr October 06, 2026 2 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

ICON Real Estate Advisors has arranged the $10.475 million sale of an 88-unit multifamily portfolio at 255 and 284 Prospect Street in East Orange, New Jersey. The transaction matters because it represents a lender-controlled disposition following a foreclosure, with the portfolio sold as an REO transaction to MNM Holding LLC. The sale also carries unusual repeat-actor dynamics: ICON Managing Partner David Oropeza exclusively represented the seller, marking the fifth time he has sold the Prospect Street properties over his career, while ICON Director, Multifamily David Betesh represented the buyer in what is their third completed transaction together.

The two properties sit along Prospect Street, described by ICON as one of East Orange's established residential corridors, and contain a mix of studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom residences. At the time of sale, approximately 51% of the portfolio was vacant. That vacancy is central to the deal's mechanics: the buyer is positioned to undertake a substantial renovation and lease-up program across the properties. Oropeza framed the vacancy and the foreclosure-driven circumstances as factors where ICON's history with the assets was particularly valuable, saying the firm was able to identify a buyer with the experience and appetite to take on the business plan while executing a transaction that met the seller's objectives.

The evidence for this transaction comes from a single full-text source, a Yield PRO article published on October 6, 2026. The source provides the sale price, property addresses, buyer and seller representation, vacancy rate, unit mix, and the broader year-to-date context for ICON's East Orange activity. It also includes direct quotes from Oropeza and Betesh. However, the dossier does not include the original press release, transaction documents, or independent confirmation from the buyer, seller, or lender. Key details such as the prior loan amount, the foreclosure timeline, the lender's identity, the per-unit price, and the buyer's renovation budget are not disclosed in the available evidence.

The sale pushes ICON's East Orange multifamily sales volume to more than $50 million year to date, encompassing approximately 12 properties across six separate transactions. That concentration suggests sustained deal flow in a specific submarket, with ICON leveraging what Oropeza called momentum across the business and assignments spanning individual assets and larger multifamily portfolios. For the broader sector, the transaction illustrates how REO multifamily assets with significant vacancy are being marketed as value-add opportunities, with brokerage relationships and repeat buyer-seller familiarity serving as deal catalysts. ICON, headquartered at 1 Boland Drive in West Orange, New Jersey, states that its partners bring more than 100 years of experience and over $20 billion in combined career transaction volume, with a multifamily investment sales focus across New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, Maryland, and Virginia.

The main limitation is the single-source nature of the reporting. The article is a secondary account from a trade publication, and the dossier flags that no material claims are corroborated by a second independent source. Missing information includes the foreclosure specifics, the seller entity's name beyond being lender-controlled, the buyer's capitalization, and any projected post-renovation rents or occupancy targets. What to watch is whether the buyer's renovation and lease-up plan materializes, whether ICON's East Orange volume continues at the current pace, and whether additional reporting or public records confirm the transaction terms and the foreclosure background.
