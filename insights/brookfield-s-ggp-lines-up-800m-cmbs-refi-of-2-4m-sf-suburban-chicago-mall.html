Brookfield's GGP Lines Up $800M CMBS Refi Of 2.4M SF Suburban Chicago Mall

A five-bank syndicate is set to close an $800M CMBS refinancing for Oakbrook Center, repaying a prior $700M loan and returning about $65M of equity to sponsors,

Ben Rohr September 23, 2026 2 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Affiliates of Brookfield's GGP are slated to close soon on a major refinancing for Oakbrook Center, a Class-A mall in Oak Brook, Illinois. The transaction matters because it tests whether top-tier regional malls can still access large-scale CMBS capital at a moment when retail real estate financing remains bifurcated between dominant properties and everything else. Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, Citibank, Goldman Sachs and Wells Fargo are identified as the originators and sellers of the $800M loan tied to the property, which is expected to close on or around Oct. 8, according to a presale report from S&P; Global. The deal's size and syndicate composition signal institutional confidence in the asset's cash flow durability.

The mechanics are straightforward but reveal meaningful equity extraction. The commercial mortgage-backed security offering will repay a previous $700M CMBS loan tied to the property, pay $30M in early payoff penalties, fund $5M in closing costs and return about $65M of equity to the sponsors. The loan has a five-year term and is interest-only for the entire term, with an assumed rate of 5.9%. That structure preserves sponsor cash flow during the loan term but leaves principal repayment to a future refinancing or sale, a risk worth noting given the five-year horizon.

The evidence supporting the deal comes from a single presale report cited by Bisnow, which draws on S&P; Global data and Placer.AI foot traffic figures. Oakbrook Center saw about 14.5 million visitors over the past year, according to Placer.AI data in the presale report, which ranks it "among the most-visited malls in the U.S." Sales for the total property have grown significantly to more than $1.2B in the trailing 12 months ending May 2026, rising 81.0% from $673M in 2019, according to the report. As of July 2026, the mall was 94.1% leased with more than 160 distinct tenants. GGP CEO Kevin McCrain said in May: "In our view, it's the best center in the country. It does well over $1,500 [per SF] in sales. We keep investing into it over and over again, because everybody wants to be there."

The sector implication is that capital remains available for dominant, well-invested mall assets even as weaker properties struggle. Since 2015, sponsorship — which includes GGP and Institutional Mall Investors — has invested more than $374M in capital expenditures and redevelopment projects at the mall, according to the report. McCrain framed Oakbrook as a national template: "Oak Brook is symbolic of how we think about things nationally. It is differentiated uses, continued investment, continued upgrades, and that sort of micro strategy for every space." The refinancing suggests lenders are willing to underwrite that strategy with large, interest-only CMBS exposure.

Several limitations temper the read-through. The analysis rests on a single secondary source summarizing one presale report; no loan documents, servicer data, or independent appraisals were reviewed. The 5.9% assumed rate and interest-only term are reported, not confirmed against final pricing. The $65M equity return and $30M early payoff penalty are deal-specific and may not reflect broader sponsor behavior. What to watch: whether the deal prices as expected, whether the five-year term creates refinancing pressure if rates stay elevated, and whether Oakbrook's sales and leasing metrics hold up after the loan closes.
