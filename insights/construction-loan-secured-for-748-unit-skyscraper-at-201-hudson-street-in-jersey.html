Construction Loan Secured For 748-Unit Skyscraper at 201 Hudson Street In Jersey City, New Jersey

Developers have secured a $277 million construction loan for 201 Hudson Street, a 69-story residential tower in Jersey City, New Jersey. The project is being developed by a joint venture between Urby and Rockpoint, with design by Concrete Amsterdam and HLW International serving as architect of record. The 677-foot-tall structure will span approximately 528,000 square feet and yield 748 rental units, along with roughly 10,000 square feet of ground-floor retail space, structured parking, and a suite of lifestyle amenities. The financing milestone matters because it signals forward movement on the second phase of a three-tower master plan that has been in progress since 2012 amendments to the Harborside Financial Center Master Plan.

The material facts point to a large, market-rate rental project with a distinct architectural identity. Renderings depict a rectilinear glass tower rising from a multi-story podium, a departure from the cantilevering, Jenga-like massing that defines the development's first phase across the street. As the northernmost of the master plan's two remaining towers, 201 Hudson will forgo offset sections entirely, while the approved third tower to the south would retain a subtler version of the original's shifts. Plans call for a 272-space parking garage within the podium with a valet option, its roof finished with an outdoor pool and a landscaped garden hill. Residences will range from studios to three-bedroom layouts, all market rate. Amenities will include a pool, a fitness center, social and coworking spaces, outdoor recreation areas, and curated food and beverage offerings. The plans also call for the greenery between the site and Harborside 5 to be upgraded with a new pedestrian walkway and park space.

The evidence comes from a single full-text source, New York YIMBY, published on September 3, 2026. The report identifies the tower as the second phase of a three-tower development. The first phase, the 69-story, 762-unit tower across the street at 200 Greene Street, was co-developed by Ironstate Development and Roseland Residential Trust and opened in 2017 as Jersey City Urby. It was later rebranded as Sable after Urby sold the property to Veris Residential, and ownership transferred to Affinius Capital earlier this year following its acquisition of Veris. This ownership history provides context for the joint venture structure behind 201 Hudson Street, though the source does not detail the equity split, loan terms, or construction timeline.

The sector implications are notable for Jersey City's multifamily pipeline. A 748-unit tower with ground-floor retail and structured parking represents a substantial addition to the Hudson waterfront rental stock. The project's design continuity with the first phase, while simplifying the massing, suggests an effort to maintain a recognizable architectural language across the master plan. The inclusion of a pedestrian walkway and park space between the site and Harborside 5 also indicates a focus on public realm improvements beyond the building itself. However, the source does not provide data on current Jersey City vacancy rates, rent levels, or absorption trends, so the market impact cannot be quantified from the available evidence.

Several limitations and unknowns remain. The dossier contains no information on the construction lender, interest rate, loan-to-cost ratio, or repayment terms. It also does not specify a groundbreaking date, completion target, or projected unit mix beyond the studio-to-three-bedroom range. The source is a secondary outlet, and the underlying financing documents or municipal approvals were not reviewed. What to watch includes whether the third tower proceeds as approved, how the retail component is leased, and whether the project's market-rate positioning holds up against broader multifamily supply dynamics in Jersey City.
