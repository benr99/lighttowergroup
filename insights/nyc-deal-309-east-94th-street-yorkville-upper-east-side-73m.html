One block from the 96th Street Q train entrance on the Upper East Side, a 145,838-square-foot pre-war commercial block-front sitting on nearly 28,000 square feet of land is one of the largest single-asset footprints left in Yorkville. On August 31, 2026, 309 East 94th Street Development LLC paid $73.14 million ($501 per square foot) to acquire 309 East 94th Street from 309 East 94th Street Property Owner LLC, edging 4.5 percent above the $70 million price recorded in December 2021.

The per-foot math explains why 309 East 94th Street Property Owner LLC chose this moment to harvest gains—and why 309 East 94th Street Development LLC was willing to pay up. At $501 a square foot across 145,838 square feet, the closing price represents a 4.5 percent appreciation over the $70 million trade recorded in December 2021.

Clearing $73.14 Million Unencumbered for 27,695 Square Feet of Yorkville Dirt

Notice the shift in the ownership entity's name: from 309 East 94th Street Property Owner LLC to 309 East 94th Street Development LLC. The $73.14 million deed cleared the City Register without a simultaneous acquisition mortgage, resetting the basis at $501 per existing square foot across the five-story, 1927-vintage structure.

Between First and Second avenues in Yorkville, the arrival of the Second Avenue Subway turned former garage and warehouse blocks into prime medical, life-science, and residential tower sites. Holding a 27,695-square-foot through-lot free and clear of legacy mortgage debt positions the sponsorship to close a dedicated construction or redevelopment facility on clean title.
