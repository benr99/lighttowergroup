Flex Secures $2B Investment for Axiom at $37.5B Enterprise Value

A $2.0 billion convertible preferred investment establishes a $37.5 billion initial enterprise value for Flex's planned AI infrastructure spin-off, Axiom Soluti

Ben Rohr October 06, 2026 2 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Flex has secured a $2.0 billion convertible preferred equity investment in Axiom Solutions International, establishing an initial enterprise value of $37.5 billion for the AI infrastructure business that Flex plans to spin off as an independent public company in the first quarter of 2027. The announcement matters because it provides an external valuation benchmark for a business Flex has assembled around high-density AI infrastructure, including embedded and critical power, electrical infrastructure, power conversion, thermal management, and compute integration. When Flex formally named Axiom in September, the preliminary Form 10 established operating scale but did not provide this external investment valuation, making the $37.5 billion figure the most consequential new data point in the announcement.

Funds affiliated with General Catalyst, Koch Equity Development, and co-investors are purchasing the Series A Convertible Preferred Stock. The financing provides equity capital for Axiom's pending acquisition of EPC Power, the $4.4 billion transaction Flex announced in September to expand its grid-to-chip power portfolio. Flex has separately arranged committed term-loan financing for the remainder of the EPC Power acquisition. The preferred shares carry a 10% annual cash dividend before separation; afterward, the terms shift to 6% cash or 7% PIK. This structure clarifies how Flex intends to fund the next major expansion of Axiom while preserving flexibility around the planned separation.

The evidence comes from a single secondary source, Converge Digest, which reported the announcement on October 6, 2026. The source is a full-text read and is the sole basis for this analysis. The report indicates that the pending EPC Power acquisition moves the Axiom portfolio farther upstream into MW-scale power conversion, grid-forming controls, energy storage, and emerging 800 VDC architectures. The financing should not be treated as completion of either the EPC Power acquisition or the Axiom separation. Both remain subject to applicable approvals and closing conditions, and Flex continues to target completion of the Axiom separation in Q1 calendar 2027, subject to required approvals and customary conditions.

For the data center and AI infrastructure sector, the investment signals that large-scale capital is being directed toward integrated power and thermal solutions for high-density compute environments. The involvement of General Catalyst and Koch Equity Development as preferred investors adds a layer of external validation to Axiom's positioning in the grid-to-chip power segment. The $37.5 billion enterprise value, while derived from a single announcement, provides a reference point for how the market may assess vertically integrated AI infrastructure businesses that combine power conversion, electrical infrastructure, and compute integration under one entity.

Several limitations apply. The dossier contains no corroborating sources, no independent confirmation of the valuation, and no details on the conversion mechanics of the preferred stock beyond the dividend terms. The source does not specify the size of the committed term-loan financing, the expected closing date for the EPC Power acquisition, or the conditions that could delay the Axiom separation. Investors should watch for the completion of the EPC Power transaction, the filing of additional separation documents, and any subsequent disclosures that confirm or revise the $37.5 billion enterprise value. Until those events occur, the valuation should be understood as an initial benchmark tied to a single financing announcement rather than a fully market-tested figure.
