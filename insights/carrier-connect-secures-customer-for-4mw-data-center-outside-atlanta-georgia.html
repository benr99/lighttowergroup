Carrier Connect secures customer for 4MW data center outside Atlanta, Georgia

Carrier Connect, via its PureColo subsidiary, leased 1001 Windward Concourse in Alpharetta from Mapletree Industrial Trust earlier this month.

Ben Rohr October 19, 2026 1 min read 1 source
Share LinkedIn X / Twitter

Carrier Connect, via its PureColo subsidiary, leased 1001 Windward Concourse in Alpharetta from Mapletree Industrial Trust earlier this month. The facility was previously solely occupied by GE.

Carter Validus acquired the facility from GE for $50 million in a 2014 sale-leaseback deal. Carter Validus then sold its entire data center portfolio to Mapletree in 2021 as part of a pivot to focus on healthcare real estate.

Mark Binns, Carrier Connect CEO, said the deal represents the first 4MW of a previously announced 25MW customer letter of intent. The customer's identity has not been disclosed.
