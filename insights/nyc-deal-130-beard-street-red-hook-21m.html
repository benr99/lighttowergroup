Red Hook Waterfront's 130 Beard Street Trades for $21.5M With Israel Discount Bank Financing

Down at the southern tip of Red Hook, where Van Brunt Street meets Beard Street across from the Erie Basin waterfront, modern brick-and-beam commercial buildings rarely hit the open market. On September 16, 2026, 411 Van Brunt Realty LLC paid $21.5 million ($1,210 per square foot) to acquire the five-story, 2018-built property at 130 Beard Street from 411 Van Brunt LLC, marking an 85.2 percent gain over the $11.61 million price recorded in November 2008.

The per-foot math explains why 411 Van Brunt LLC chose this moment to harvest gains—and why 411 Van Brunt Realty LLC was willing to pay up. At $1,210 a square foot across 17,771 square feet, the closing price represents a 85.2 percent appreciation over the $11.61 million trade recorded in November 2008.

Israel Discount Bank Finances a Low-Leverage Red Hook Recapitalization

Alongside the $21.5 million deed transfer, Israel Discount Bank of New York recorded a $2.3 million supplemental mortgage on September 18, 2026, putting new-money recorded debt at 10.7 percent of the purchase valuation against $19.2 million of implied equity.

Sitting on a 23,400-square-foot waterfront lot next to Red Hook's IKEA and the Van Brunt dining corridor, the 2018-vintage five-story structure commands unobstructed Upper Bay views that cannot be replicated under current M1-2 coastal height limits.
