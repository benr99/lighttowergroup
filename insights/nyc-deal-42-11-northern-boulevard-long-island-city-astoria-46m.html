Tishman Speyer & Silverstein Capital Partners Shells Out $46.2M for Long Island City / Astoria's 42-11 Northern Boulevard With Fannie Mae DUS Financing

In a market where bid-ask spreads have kept dozens of New York City investment sales on ice, Tishman Speyer & Silverstein Capital Partners via Astoria Gateway Multifamily Owner LLC stepped in to close on 42-11 Northern Boulevard in Long Island City / Astoria, Queens for $46,200,000 ($46.2M). The fee-simple deed transfer, which closed on 2026-03-22 and hit the city register on 2026-03-29, prices the 115,500-square-foot elevator multifamily & retail mixed-use at $400/GSF, marking a 140.0% step-up over the $19,250,000 basis recorded in 2020 (6.1-year hold). Across the property's 132 residential apartments, the closing price equates to $350,000/unit and a verified 5.85% cap rate.

The Deal & The Players

Tishman Speyer & Silverstein Capital Partners via Astoria Gateway Multifamily Owner LLC acquired the 100% interest from Criterion Group (Shibber Khan) via Northern Blvd Queens Holdings LLC, with Newmark (investment sales and agency debt placement) advising on the trade. ACRIS Document ID 2026032900511001 records the $46,200,000 sale of 42-11 Northern Boulevard (BBL 4-00678-0001) on the Long Island City/Astoria border in Queens from Northern Blvd Queens Holdings LLC (Criterion Group, led by Shibber Khan) to Astoria Gateway Multifamily Owner LLC (Tishman Speyer). The 9-story, 115,500-square-foot D6 elevator apartment building built in 2023 contains 132 residential units and ground-floor retail under M1-4/R7X zoning adjacent to the 46th Street R/M subway station, trading at $400 per gross square foot ($350,000 per unit) and a 5.85% cap rate. Simultaneously recorded mortgage 2026032900511002 shows a $30,030,000 Fannie Mae DUS agency loan arranged by Newmark at 65% LTV alongside $16,170,000 of implied equity.

The Capital Stack X-Ray

To fund the acquisition, the buyer locked in a $30,030,000 senior mortgage (ACRIS Document ID 2026032900511002) from Fannie Mae DUS (via Newmark Multifamily Capital Markets), representing an implied loan-to-value ratio of 65% alongside $16,170,000 of sponsor equity. Sitting behind the senior note is $16,170,000 in implied equity ($16.17M), illustrating the disciplined 60% to 70% senior attachment points that are actually clearing New York City credit committees in 2026.

The Asset & Street Reality

Identified on the city tax map as BBL 4-00678-0001 under building class D6 (Elevator Multifamily & Retail Mixed-Use), 42-11 Northern Boulevard spans 115,500 gross square feet across 9 stories on a 23,100-square-foot lot zoned M1-4/R7X (MX-2) (built 2023).

The Desk Read

On our placement desk at Light Tower Group, 42-11 Northern Boulevard is a textbook readout of how institutional capital is pricing New York City risk right now. First, clearing basis is everything: at $400/GSF, the sponsorship is buying Long Island City / Astoria footage at a basis that insulates the business plan against rate volatility. Second, look at the debt execution: Fannie Mae DUS (via Newmark Multifamily Capital Markets) stepping up for $30.03M at 65% LTV proves that credit committees are wide open for right-sized basis and institutional sponsorship—especially when borrowers use CEMA structures or agency/life-company execution to protect day-one cash yield.
