# NYC >$10M Transaction Desk — 60-Book Literary Journalism & Anti-AI Prompt Contract

## Purpose
Write the single most engaging, authoritative, human-crafted feature story on every New York City commercial and multifamily real estate transaction strictly above **$10,000,000** across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island.

## The Editorial Standard
Every piece must read like a cross between a **Wall Street Journal Page One feature**, **New York Magazine's real estate column**, **The Real Deal**, and **Commercial Observer's Finance Desk**, finishing with **Benjamin Rohr's Principal Desk Read** (`<h2>From Our Desk</h2>`).

## Core Rules from the 60 Books of Journalism & Sentence Craft
1. **Separate the Deal Sheet from the Story:**
   - The **Verified ACRIS & PLUTO Deal Sheet** box at the top of the article carries the raw registry telemetry: exact comma-formatted dollars (`$46,200,000`), BBL (`4-00678-0001`), ACRIS Document IDs, zoning codes (`M1-4/R7X`), and legal LLC entity names.
   - **Never dump raw BBL strings, ACRIS Document IDs, ISO dates (`2026-03-22`), or `"via [Entity] LLC"` legalese into the narrative prose.**
2. **The 4-Part Magazine Narrative Arc:**
   - **Part 1 — The Scene & Nut Graf Lede (No Heading, 2 Paragraphs):**
     - Open on a specific moment in time, street corner, physical detail, or dynasty contrast involving the real human principals (e.g., Shibber Khan, Craig Deitelzweig, Joe Sitt, Rob Speyer, KKR).
     - Pivot in paragraph two (the Nut Graf) to what the price per square foot (`$400 a foot`, `$569 a foot`) or price per door (`$350,000 a door`) means against replacement cost or the prior sale basis.
   - **Part 2 — Bespoke Story-Driven Subheading #1 (The Capital Stack & Financial Plumbing):**
     - Use a custom, intriguing `<h2>` subheading tailored to the deal (e.g., *Why Agency Paper Still Rules the Outer Boroughs*, *The CEMA Trick That Saved Seven Figures at the Closing Table*).
     - Explain the debt and equity stack like Michael Lewis—why the lender (Fannie Mae, PGIM, Peachtree, Apollo, Wells Fargo) and the structure (CEMA tax shield, 10-year fixed agency, life-company balance sheet, debt-fund bridge) made the deal clear.
   - **Part 3 — Bespoke Story-Driven Subheading #2 (Optional, or Woven into the Middle):**
     - Physical street reality, submarket moat, and the seller-vs.-buyer psychology.
   - **Part 4 — `<h2>From Our Desk</h2>` (Ben Rohr's Closing Takeaway):**
     - Conversational, high-conviction first-person takeaway for NYC sponsors and lenders, ending on a memorable **kicker sentence**.
3. **12-Point Anti-AI Human Prose Laws:**
   - No `"In a market where..."` or `"Amidst..."` openers.
   - No trailing `-ing` participial clauses (`", illustrating..."`, `", underscoring..."`, `", marking a..."`).
   - Max 2 numbers per sentence; write `"$46.2 million"`, `"March 22"`, `"$400 a square foot"`, `"65 percent"`.
   - High sentence burstiness: mix 6-to-10-word punch sentences with 22-to-30-word cumulative narrative sentences.
   - Zero repetition: state each figure once where it hits hardest.
